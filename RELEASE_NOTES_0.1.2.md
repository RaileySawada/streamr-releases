## Streamr v0.1.2

### Fullscreen playback

- The fullscreen player fills the display and hides the Streamr headings and helper row.
- Series playback shows a compact Next episode button only during the final 30 seconds when the player reports its progress.
- Press Esc to exit fullscreen; a 3.5-second hint appears when fullscreen starts.
- Fixed fullscreen permission for embedded and redirected HTTPS video frames.
- TMDB does not provide intro or outro timestamps, so timed skip buttons are not shown.

### Installer details

- Added Railey Dela Peña as the publisher shown in Windows installed-app details.
- This update keeps the existing Streamr app identity and user library.

Install over v0.1.1 or use the in-app update notification to install this release.
